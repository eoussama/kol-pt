Why it exists

Patreon started taking down copyrighted content, so KOL stopped naming the shows he reacts to in his posts. This extension adds that information back, right where you watch: under each post on Patreon.

Features

  • Reactions on every post

    A panel under each tracked post lists what is reacted to, when it starts and how long it lasts.

  • Jump to a reaction

    Start the post's video at any reaction, or pick up where you left off.

  • Details and links

    Synopsis, genres and alternative titles, with links to MyAnimeList, AniList, Kitsu, IMDb and YouTube.

  • Watched and favorites

    Sign in to tick off what you've watched and keep favorites, across devices and tabs.

  • Browse from the toolbar

    Search every tracked stream and every show from the extension's popup.

  • Fix it together

    Flag a wrong timestamp, a missing reaction or an untracked post, and moderators fix it for everyone.

How to use it

  1. Install the extension for your browser.
  2. Open KOL's Patreon posts: tracked posts get a reactions panel under their tags.
  3. Click a reaction to start the video there, or open the extension's popup to browse.

You need access to KOL's posts on Patreon to watch them: the extension does not unlock anything.
